# Reproducibility framework

## FAIR data

Findable, accessible, interoperable, reusable. The FAIR guiding principles
([Wilkinson et al., *Sci. Data* 2016](https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2016.18))
set out what published research data has to satisfy to be useful to anyone
other than the group that recorded it. The pages that follow are what each
principle costs, and buys, in materials TEM.
